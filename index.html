About me

I am a Senior Machine Learning Research Engineer in Bloomberg AI in New York, where I am part of the AI Search team and work on multimodal document understanding: retrieval, extraction and agentic reasoning over text-heavy and visually rich documents such as filings, reports, presentations, tables and charts.

On AI Search, I work on multimodal document retrieval; our research on this is published as MIDR (EMNLP 2026). Previously, I was a member of the core agent team behind ASKB, the agentic conversational interface on the Bloomberg Terminal, where I worked on conversation memory and context management and on ASKB Workflows. I am interested in vision-based retrievers for enterprise-scale Document AI and in memory systems that help agents manage context across long interactions.

I enjoy taking research all the way to production. I work with engineers, researchers, product managers and financial analysts, and mentor research interns. I also collaborate closely with Prof. Hamed Zamani (UMass Amherst, CIIR) and Prof. Mohit Bansal (UNC Chapel Hill). Before this I led the AI organization for Moody's Analytics' KYC business, was a Senior Research Scientist in Bloomberg AI, and was a Senior Research Associate at Infosys in Palo Alto. I have also served as Adjunct Faculty at IIIT-Delhi. Earlier research covered keyphrase extraction, summarization and computational social science.

I hold a PhD in Information Science from the University of Arkansas at Little Rock, and BS and MS degrees in Computer Science from Banaras Hindu University, India. I speak Bengali, Hindi and English, and a little Sanskrit. Outside work I play the tabla and cricket, and enjoy music, food and travel.

Current research

Multimodal document understanding

Reading and extracting information from visually rich documents with multimodal LLMs, including document layout, tables, charts and multi-page, multi-document question answering.

Retrieval & RAG

Multimodal and vision-based retrieval, enrichment-augmented indexing, hybrid sparse and dense retrieval, and retrieval-augmented generation over large document collections.

Agentic AI & memory

Agents that plan and act over research workflows, tool use and MCP, and conversation memory and context management for long user–assistant interactions.
